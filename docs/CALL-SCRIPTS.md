# Call scripts — NGSA, MCO, NUPRC

Copy these. Fill the bracketed bits. **No payment language.** If they mention a fee, write it down and say you will follow their procedure — do not offer money unprompted.

Office culture: call first, email the same day opening with “further to our telephone conversation this morning”. That is what gets logged.

---

You are in Abuja. Utako is a short trip. A call plus a visit beats three weeks of email.

### 30 seconds (switchboard / PA)

> Good morning. My name is **[Usman Ahmad]**. I am calling about access to the National Mineral Occurrence Database. Could I speak with **Dr. Kwache** at the National Geosciences Information Centre, or with whoever handles data requests at the GeoData Centre?

If they ask what it is about:

> We are building a Nigerian mineral intelligence platform that already runs on Mining Cadastre Office titles and NEITI 2023 production figures. Every dataset is credited to the originating agency. The piece we are missing is NGSA’s occurrence database — with coordinates — and we would like to be told the proper procedure.

### If you are put through (keep it under two minutes)

> Thank you for taking the call. We would like to request:
>
> 1. the **National Mineral Occurrence Database** — ideally a CSV or Excel export, **with geographic coordinates** for each occurrence;
> 2. any **regional geochemical** datasets you can release (the gold, copper, silver maps and the 2024 index);
> 3. **airborne geophysics** coverage — even just the sheet index and the priority belts of Zamfara/Kebbi, Nasarawa/Plateau, Kogi/Kwara, Osun/Oyo, if a national dump is not practical.
>
> We will complete any forms, sign a data-use agreement, and credit NGSA on every screen that uses the data. I can come to Utako to collect it on a USB drive if that is easier.
>
> Could you tell me who I should write to, and whether a visit this week is possible?

### Three things to get before you hang up

1. A **name, email and direct number** for the officer who handles the request.
2. The **procedure** — form, letter to the DG, in-person only, timeline.
3. **Permission to visit**, and what to bring (letter-headed printout, ID, empty USB).

If they raise a fee or a commercial tariff:

> Thank you, I will follow whatever procedure the Agency uses. Could you point me to the form or the officer who handles that so I write it correctly?

Then stop. Do not bargain. Do not offer to pay.

### Same day

Send Email 1 (or the follow-up in `FOLLOW-UP.md` if Email 1 already went on 17 August), opening:

> Further to my telephone conversation with **[name]** this morning, I am putting our request in writing as discussed.

If nobody picks up: visit Utako between 10:00 and 14:00 with a letter-headed printout of Email 1. In-person requests are logged; email often is not.

## After any call

Write down, immediately:

- Date, time, number dialled
- Name and title of whoever spoke
- Procedure they described
- Anything they said about forms, fees, format, or a visit
- The email you promised to send, and when

That note is what makes the follow-up email a record rather than a cold ask.
